About CALIP

Cognitive Atomic Legal Intelligence Platform

Architectural specification, provenance principles, and safety guardrails.

Core Provenance Principles

  • Source Grounding: Answers are produced exclusively from verified document evidence. If documents do not establish an answer, the system states so explicitly.
  • Exact Citations: Answers link to Case, Document Title, Court, and raw PDF Page Numbers.
  • Cryptographic Integrity: Ingested PDFs preserve original SHA-256 hashes to prevent duplicate ingestion and verify authenticity.
  • Pluggable OCR Architecture: Supports digital text extraction, external user OCR scripts via MY_OCR_COMMAND, and OpenCV/Tesseract image pre-processing.
  • No Outcome Fabrication: The platform is an information and document research intelligence system, not an automatic court-outcome prediction engine.

Search Engine & AI Discovery

Every public case, document, judgment, and order is rendered as server-side HTML with JSON-LD Schema.org structured data. Search engines and AI crawlers can discover and index all legal text without requiring client-side JavaScript execution.
