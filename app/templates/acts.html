Indian Penal Code, 1860

IPC

Substantive criminal law defining offenses against property, breach of trust, cheating, forgery, and conspiracy.

Common Sections Cited:
• Section 406 : Criminal breach of trust
• Section 409 : Criminal breach of trust by public servant, banker, merchant or agent
• Section 420 : Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property
• Section 468 / 471 : Forgery for purpose of cheating & using forged document as genuine
• Section 120-B : Criminal conspiracy

Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973

Procedural law governing police investigation, supply of prosecution copies, bail, trial procedure, and quashing.

Common Sections Cited:
• Section 207 : Supply to the accused of copy of police report and other documents
